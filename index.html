Overview

A bilingual website for a residential brand. It presents the brand's properties, branches and units, and includes online booking, a Corporate Stays section and a partnership page (Grow with RHB). All site content is managed from a custom admin panel, without touching code.

What I built

Properties & bookings
  • Property and unit management: specs, features, landmarks, locations, neighbourhood cards and stats
  • Booking system for rooms and units
  • Contact and partnership inquiry handling
Custom CMS
  • Block-based pages with sliders, tabs and sections
  • Configurable navbar and footer
  • News and blog with categories, tags, archive, search, views and shares
  • Form builder, polls and surveys
Platform
  • Roles and permissions with Gates & Policies
  • Full Arabic/English localization with RTL/LTR
  • PDF export, email notifications and REST API

My contribution

I built the project on my own and managed it from start to delivery: planning the work, developing the website and admin panel, and handling the project with the client.
